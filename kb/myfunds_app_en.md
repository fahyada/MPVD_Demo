# TISCO My Funds User Guide for Provident Fund Members (English)

## [p.1] Cover: TISCO My Funds — check your provident fund balance today (English Version)
(หน้าปก) "English Version" — same cover artwork as the Thai version (text on the cover is in Thai). "TISCO My Funds ตรวจสอบยอดเงินกองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ* ได้แล้ววันนี้" (TISCO My Funds: check your provident fund balance, available today) — "บริการสำหรับสมาชิกกองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ ภายใต้บลจ.ทิสโก้" (service for provident fund members under TISCO Asset Management). ## [p.2] Change Language — steps 1–2 (+ language screens)
1. Choose "Menu" (centre TISCO icon on the bottom bar of the News screen)
2. In the menu, under section "กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ" (Provident Fund), choose "EN/TH"
3. On the "เปลี่ยนภาษา" (Change Language) screen — "ท่านสามารถเลือกภาษาได้ดังนี้ / you can set a language as below." — switch from "ภาษาไทย" to "English". The screen then shows "Change Language" with "Back", options "Thai" / "English" (English selected).

Menu screen contents (Thai labels):
- กองทุนรวม (Mutual funds): "พอร์ตลงทุน" (Portfolio), "ซื้อขาย" (Buy/Sell), "เปิดบัญชี" (Open account), "แผนการลงทุน" (Investment plan), "สถานะรายการ" (Transaction status), "รายการย้อนหลัง" (Transaction history), "สรุปการลงทุนรายปี" (Annual investment summary), "กองทุน" (Funds)
- กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ (Provident fund): "รายงาน" (Report), "EN/TH"
- อื่นๆ (Others): "ข่าวสาร" (News), "สิทธิพิเศษ" (Privileges), "ตั้งค่า" (Settings), "ติดต่อ บลจ." (Contact TISCO Asset)

Bottom navigation bar (English mode): "News", "Fund Info", "Menu", "Portfolio", "Transaction"

## [p.3] First Register for Foreigner — steps 1–5
1. Choose "Portfolio" (bottom bar of the News screen)
2. Choose "Log into e-Provident Fund" — on the "เข้าสู่ระบบ" (Log in) screen select "กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ" (Log into e-Provident Fund); the other option is "กองทุนรวม" (Log into e-Invest / MyFunds)
3. Register using the same USER/PASSWORD as myPVD or e-Provident Fund. — "Provident Fund" login screen (TH/EN toggle): "Username", "Password", "Sign in"; "Still have no account?" > "Register"; "Forgot Username/Password"; "Help"
4. Verification by TAX ID — "Verification" screen: "Tax Identification No. *" ("Please enter your 13-digit Tax Identification No."), then "Confirm"
5. CREATE a "PIN" — "Create a PIN for Login" screen (6-digit PIN keypad)

## [p.4] Login to My Fund for Foreigner — steps 1–2 (subsequent logins)
1. Choose "Portfolio" (bottom bar)
2. Please enter your PIN to log in — screen "Please enter your PIN to log in" (6-digit keypad, "Back", link "Forgot PIN")

## [p.6] Download i-Statement — steps 1–3
1. Click Here — tap the report (document) icon at the top right of the portfolio screen (next to the gear icon)
2. Choses [Choose] Report — "Statement" screen > "Individual Statement": select the statement period (example buttons "31 Dec 25", "30 Jun 25"); a preview of the statement is shown (page "1 จาก 2")
3. Download — tap the download/share icon at the bottom right of the statement preview
